Product Requirements Document

Soft Login: Deferring Account Creation Past First Reward

Move account linking from a hard wall right after the tutorial to a moment players actually have something to protect, to recover the players a forced login costs before they've decided they like the game.

StatusDraft, ready for review
OwnerAbhinav Kotar
AreaGrowth, onboarding funnel
SourceFunnel teardown, Sep 2026
01

Problem

A cohort analysis of 10,000 simulated installs on a mobile F2P RPG funnel found a 46% drop at account linking, the single largest loss of any step, well above a ~20% genre benchmark for this step. Crash logs and OAuth completion rates ruled out a technical cause. Review text and the exact drop-off point pointed to timing: players are asked to create an account before they've had a reason to want one.

02

Goal

Lift tutorial-to-linked-account conversion from 33% toward 50% by removing the login ask before the payoff, without hurting D7 retention or reopening a data-loss risk for players who never link.

03

Process — how we got here

Ran the full funnel, not just the headline metricInstall through Day-1 return, to find where the biggest bar wasn't the real problem.
Ruled out the technical explanation firstChecked error rates, OAuth completion, and device clustering before trusting the trust story.
Split by acquisition source to stress-test the hypothesisOrganic vs. paid UA conversion gap confirmed this behaves like a trust problem, not a bug.
Designed the fix around the trigger, not a delayTied the ask to a loss-aversion moment, a rare pull or long session, instead of a fixed timer.
Defined guardrails before scoping the experimentNamed the new risk the fix introduces, uninstall before linking, so it's tracked from day one.
04

Solution

What changes

  • No login prompt at tutorial end or first reward
  • Prompt fires on first loss-aversion moment: rare pull, purchase, or 10+ min session
  • Default option is one-tap platform sign-in, not email or OTP

What doesn't

  • Guest play stays fully playable and uninterrupted
  • Prompt shown at most 3 times, then goes quiet
  • Manual "link account" stays available in profile anytime
05

Success metrics

Primary: tutorial-to-linked-account conversion, 33% → ~50%.
Guardrails: D7 retention flat or up; uninstall-before-link rate tracked as a new risk.

06

Rollout & non-goals

Device-level 50/50 split on new installs only, 10% soft launch first, two-week guardrail read. Out of scope for this change: redesigning the tutorial itself, monetization prompts, or existing linked accounts.

Portfolio PRD, written against a simulated dataset modeled on public F2P onboarding benchmarks. Companion teardown and screen mockups available separately.
